# Credits

## Exercise illustrations

The exercise drawings in `Trainer/Resources/ExercisePhotos/` are line drawings from **Everkinetic** (everkinetic.com, created by Greg Priday), obtained through the open-source **wger** project (wger.de). One drawing is by wger.de. They're used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence listed for each image. The only change is placing the start and end positions side by side on a white background. Adapted images are shared under the same licence.

Where no matching drawing was available, the app substitutes a drawing of a closely related movement (column 2). Exercises without an openly licensed drawing ship with no picture. Users can add their own photos in the app.
